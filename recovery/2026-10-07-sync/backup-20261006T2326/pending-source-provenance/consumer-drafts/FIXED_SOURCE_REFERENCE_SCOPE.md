# Fixed-source Reference boundary

Status: UNVERIFIED source draft. No strict source or imported-axiom check is claimed.

The intended source is the literal remembered local source S. Its native proof,
shading lower, and union upper must concern that same source. The public
`exists_reference_from_volume_bounds` derives the real near-multiplicity lower
from those volume estimates and their scalar payment; it does not choose a new
extremizing source or accept the desired multiplicity as a premise.

The output `ref.original = cells` is proved using uniqueness of finite cubical
presentations at the positive mesh S.thickness/2. Hence E1, and every subsequent
incidence restriction such as a finite rank selection, keeps its original tube
and cell index. A witness attached to the original cell height k(3), including
its remembered old-height choice and hereditary higher-plane/quotient upper or
error statements, survives exactly by restriction.

This boundary ends at the next mesoscopic map. Its `rawHeight` and
`translatedHeight` can identify distinct original cell heights; the fixed-source
Reference theorem does not show that either new label determines the inherited
higher field. A common higher field on such a later height requires a genuine
factorization of the actual maps, or another actual one-old-height selection.

The remembered-height framework is reusable at a later scale only after proving
its same-source counting inputs and the AD bounds of its unchanged complete
reference slices at that scale. An arbitrary cut does not inherit dense higher-X
fibers, higher-Y AD lower bounds, whole point fibers, or later-scale profiles.

The bounded higher chart is independent of this issue. The original paper defines
Mat(k,l) using entries in [-1,1] before Definition 17.2, and Definition 17.4 uses
that same Mat. The existing Step3 quantization also accepts unit coefficient
bounds. No unproved 1/4 bound on the actual planar angle is used.
