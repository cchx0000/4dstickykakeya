## Fixed choices and costs

The finite history size, horizontal menu cardinality and third caller relation count precede tau/source. The rank hierarchy precedes the relative engine. The engine precedes tau, seed, g, L and L2. All source cutoffs and L3 precede D. Actual relation values may read the already fixed source, stopping depth, graph and pre-third subset.

The third count is exactly `refinementCost (d3+2) (Jhorizontal+1) L3`: one internal old-grain relation and one internal original-point relation accompany the caller relations. Q3 is `radix Spre.card L3`, and the raw budget is uniform over every eventual nonempty original sub-incidence.

The chart/height cost is `343^(Khalf+1) * 257^16 * 3^(Khalf+1)`. The full weighted quotient plus height bridge costs `quotientCost q = 4 * ceil(quotientCap q)`. The generalized pre-third cost Cpre is explicit. Its X coefficient is `(Cpre / quotientCost q) * fiberCoefficient`; it is not silently paid by the base quotient allowance. `NativeXYPreLossBudget.xyConstant_pre_loss` also proves the exact XY multiplier `max 1 (Cpre / Cbase)`.

The total height field is chosen once from the original graph's full quotient/height source, before Spre and T. It is zero off the used translated heights. Its exact raw-height readback and the derived translated metric estimate survive by inclusion. Raw spatial height and translated column height are never identified.

- The hook permits any paid Spre, hence in particular the actual whole-point coherence subset. It does not construct that coherence subset itself.
- The candidate/extra paid XY endpoints now return a uniform `NativePaidThirdBudget.HasBudget` on the exact same pre-D L3 and delta3. It proves universal raw H3 and the source-derived base X/XY/fixed-geometry power bounds. The final same-T AD monotonicity/readback is being joined; arbitrary coherence loss Cpre remains explicit.
- Fixed-height XY/Y AD does not establish a coarse-height union estimate.
- No X-fiber AD is assumed. The Y theorem uses the actual sharp global X lower and the actual same-T ambient XY AD.
- No point or tube is replaced by a rounded direction, no second parent/source is chosen, and no sparse native re-admission is used.
- These endpoints do not prove kappa=0 or the unconditional original sticky Kakeya theorem.
