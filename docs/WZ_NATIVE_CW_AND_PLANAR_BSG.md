# Original-family CW concentration and the planar additive endpoint

This checkpoint proves two complete native finite callers and the physical
phase identities needed for the next configuration branch. It does not
complete the final volume theorem.

## Original-family concentration

`scalar_original_CW_concentration` and `planar_original_CW_concentration`
start with the literal original finite tube family, its global convex-set
Wolff counting law, the original incidences and fine-direction populations,
and its original carrier regularity and global cardinality bounds.

They select actual original endpoints at one original height, construct the
point/fine-label-to-ancestor map, derive its position and direction fibers,
and retain every original tube in each occupied carrier ancestor. These full
carrier fibers are disjoint. Same-point direction offsets are preserved;
there is no assumed common offset across different source points.

The adapted root box is the actual affine box from the physical walk.
Its convexity, measurability and volume are proved internally. The residual
coordinate map is measure-preserving, giving volume `(2L)^4 rho sigma^2`
for a=1 and `(2L)^4 rho^2 sigma` for a=2. The caller specializes the original
global counting law to this box and derives the concentration lower bound.
No target-box count or volume certificate is an input.

The carrier hypothesis is the literal occupied parameter-cell lower bound.
A ball upper bound is not substituted for it. Bounded graph segments and the
original global tube count are explicit, because neither follows from local
carrier regularity alone.

## Actual planar asymmetric BSG

The planar endpoint starts with original bounded R² sets, Euclidean
separation and literal Euclidean near-sum energy. A finer deterministic grid
preserves the original labels. An actual 49-element discrepancy menu derives
the exact Z² additive energy. The finite group construction then returns
coupled original subsets, not independent coordinate selections.

The original planar iterated sums have their own actual grid-cover bound.
Uniform dyadic absorption gives `nu^K delta^epsilon` retention and all
iterated cover bounds, with the threshold chosen before nu and the sum
multiplicities. This supplies the vector-valued analogue required by the
Section21.2 use of asymmetric BSG. The scalar real theorem alone did not
justify that use.

## Physical phase relation and open localization

The signed original W phase identity is derived from actual original
incidences. Its normalized coordinates reflect xi and divide it by
`K rho`, keeping the anisotropic factor explicit. This corrects the sign and
scale bookkeeping without assuming a phase displacement estimate.

The resulting phase-graph population, the micro-scale field-to-population
bound, and the common quantitative projection still require their actual
callers. In particular, phase cardinality alone does not imply quantitative
preservation under projection. These boundaries are recorded in the
[Section21 audit](WZ_SECTION21_NATIVE_CALLER_AUDIT.md).
