## The three cases cannot be conflated

Proposition 17.2 constructs a k-planar ell-linear configuration. In dimension 4 its possible pairs are

- (3,3): Proposition 17.3, Sections 19–20
- (2,2): Proposition 17.4, Section 21
- (3,2): Proposition 17.5, Section 22

The actual source proof uses all three propositions. Full global direction coverage does not by itself force the local configuration into (3,3). Proving only the finite Kaufman input, only Proposition 17.3, or only a native transverse-menu growth lemma would therefore not finish the four-dimensional volume theorem.
