## Consistent anisotropic normalization

The source defines its bounded F using division by delta^-eta*rho, but divides the xi-coordinate of A only by rho. Its displayed (172) then misses a factor delta^-eta in the normal component. Keep K>=1 explicit and normalize instead by

    phase(p) = ((x(p)-x0)/rho, -(xi(p)-xi0)/(K*rho)),
    graph(z) = ((z-z0)/rho, (f(z)-f(z0))/(K*rho)).

Here f(z)=F(z)(1) in the scalar tangent model. Then original W witnesses give

    phase(p2')-phase(p1') = c*(graph(z1)-graph(z0)) + O(rho),

There is no need to pay K as a physical error after this normalization; its covering/population costs remain explicit.

## The next phase-window and projection obligations

Even two arbitrarily close values can straddle a grid boundary.

The printed proof splits at one scale rho=delta^(1/4), while the printed Lemma21.1 assumption quantifies over a range of rho. A fixed-scale failure does not imply all-scale anti-affine avoidance: small rapid oscillations can defeat a tiny-scale affine tube yet lie in a much thicker large-scale tube. The minimal repair seems to be proving the fixed-scale version of21.1, since its actual ABC argument uses anti-affine avoidance only at the chosen rho.
